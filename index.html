Stirling Perrott — Student Number: 34968253

Light pollution is an increasingly significant environmental issue in Australia, affecting ecosystems, wildlife behaviour, human health, and the visibility of the night sky. As urban areas expand and artificial lighting intensifies, the natural darkness that many species rely on is disrupted, particularly in coastal regions where sensitive habitats exist. Understanding how light is distributed across Australia helps reveal where ecological pressures are highest and where conservation efforts can be most effective.
